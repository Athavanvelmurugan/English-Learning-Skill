# English Mistakes and Learnings Journal

## 🔄 Active Mistakes (Require Practice & Re-testing)

### 1. Preposition + Verb Form (`preposition + verb -ing`)
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 4 — Verb Forms & Prepositions
- **Error Count:** 4 times
- **Mistake:** ❌ Before create a PR / Before merge the pull request
- **Correction:** ✅ Before **creating** a PR / Before **merging** the pull request.
- **Rule:** When a verb immediately follows a preposition (*on, in, at, after, before, about, by*), use the **gerund (`verb + -ing`)**. Example: *before creating*, *before merging*.
- **Session #15 Success:** ✅ Applied correctly in sentence (*Before working...*) AND drill (*Before creating a pull request...*).

### 2. Time Expression Naturalness (`Yesterday` vs `Previous Day`)
- **Severity:** `P3 — Minor / Style`
- **Category:** Category 19 — Natural Expression
- **Error Count:** 1 time
- **Mistake:** ❌ Previous Day, I learned Spring Boot.
- **Correction:** ✅ **Yesterday**, I learned Spring Boot.
- **Rule:** Use **`Yesterday`** when referring directly to the day before today at the start of a sentence. Use *the previous day* only when linking two past events.

### 3. Articles `a/an` Placed Before Action Verbs
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 9 — Articles & Parts of Speech
- **Error Count:** 1 time
- **Mistake:** ❌ i an implement the Playwright framework
- **Correction:** ✅ I am **implementing** the Playwright framework.
- **Rule:** Articles (`a/an`) are ONLY placed before **singular nouns** (*an API*, *an issue*). NEVER place articles before action verbs (*implement*, *execute*).

### 4. Verb Stacking & Redundant Action Words
- **Severity:** `P3 — Minor / Style`
- **Category:** Category 14 — Word Order & Natural Expression
- **Error Count:** 1 time
- **Mistake:** ❌ he is processing the playwright writing execution
- **Correction:** ✅ He is **executing** the Playwright test suite.
- **Rule:** Avoid stacking multiple action words (*processing writing execution*). Choose **one precise technical action verb** (*executing*, *running*, *writing*).

## ✅ Resolved Mistakes (Mastered)

### 1. Present Perfect vs Simple Past (Time Markers)
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 5 — Tenses
- **Resolved Date:** 2026-09-15
- **Original Error:** ❌ Yesterday, our team have executed all test scenarios. / Yesterday, I implement...
- **Mastered Fix:** ✅ **Yesterday**, I **implemented** the API framework (Simple Past V2 with time word). **I have completed** the documentation (Present Perfect without time word).

### 2. Zero Article with Framework & Tool Names (`Spring Boot`, `FastAPI`)
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 9 — Articles (Zero Article)
- **Resolved Date:** 2026-09-15
- **Original Error:** ❌ I learned the springboot. / I studied the Playwright.
- **Mastered Fix:** ✅ Standalone framework names take **zero article**: **I learned Spring Boot**, **I am learning FastAPI**.

### 3. Infinitive Pattern (`to + base verb`)
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 4 — Verb Forms & Infinitives
- **Resolved Date:** 2026-09-15 (Re-tested & Confirmed)
- **Original Error:** ❌ for proceed / to automated
- **Mastered Fix:** ✅ Use `to + base verb` (`to proceed`, `to deploy`, `to optimize`, `to automate`).

### 4. Subject-Verb Agreement with Plural & Uncountable Nouns (`AI is`, `developers ARE`)
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 2 — Subject-Verb Agreement
- **Resolved Date:** 2026-09-15
- **Original Error:** ❌ developers is fixing... / Artificial Intelligence are...
- **Mastered Fix:** ✅ Plural nouns take **are** (`developers ARE fixing`), uncountable nouns take singular **is** (`Artificial Intelligence IS transforming`).

### 5. Preposition after "Explain" (`explain ... to me`)
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 10 — Prepositions
- **Resolved Date:** 2026-09-15
- **Original Error:** ❌ Explain me Taxi system architecture.
- **Mastered Fix:** ✅ Explain the architecture **to me** (`explain + [something] + to + [someone]`).

### 6. Definite Article 'the' with Specific Noun Phrases (`the Playwright framework`)
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 9 — Articles
- **Resolved Date:** 2026-09-15
- **Original Error:** ❌ Have you checked playwright framework file structure?
- **Mastered Fix:** ✅ Specific compound noun phrases take **the** (`the Playwright framework file structure`).

### 7. Uncountable Nouns in Software Engineering (`code`)
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 6 — Nouns (Uncountable)
- **Resolved Date:** 2026-09-04
- **Original Error:** ❌ ...git pushed the codes.
- **Mastered Fix:** ✅ ...modified the **code** (never *codes*).

### 8. Comparison Words (`than` vs `then`)
- **Severity:** `P3 — Minor`
- **Category:** Category 14 — Word Order & Comparison
- **Resolved Date:** 2026-09-04
- **Original Error:** ❌ Playwright is faster then Selenium...
- **Mastered Fix:** ✅ Playwright is faster **than** Selenium... (*than* for comparison).
