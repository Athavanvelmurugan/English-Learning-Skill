# Active Mistakes (Require Practice & Re-testing)

### 1. Preposition + Verb Form (`verb + -ing` after prepositions)
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 4 — Verb Forms & Prepositions
- **Error Count:** 3 times
- **Mistake:** ❌ focusing on to enhance / before proceed an implementation plan
- **Correction:** ✅ focusing on **enhancing** / before **proceeding with** an implementation plan.
- **Rule:** When a verb immediately follows a preposition (*on, in, at, after, before, about, by*), use the **gerund (`verb + -ing`)**. Example: *focusing on enhancing*, *before proceeding*.

### 2. Articles `a/an` Placed Before Action Verbs
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 9 — Articles & Parts of Speech
- **Error Count:** 1 time
- **Mistake:** ❌ i an implement the Playwright framework
- **Correction:** ✅ I am **implementing** the Playwright framework.
- **Rule:** Articles (`a/an`) are ONLY placed before **singular nouns** (*an API*, *an issue*). NEVER place articles before action verbs (*implement*, *execute*).

### 3. Verb Stacking & Redundant Action Words
- **Severity:** `P3 — Minor / Style`
- **Category:** Category 14 — Word Order & Natural Expression
- **Error Count:** 1 time
- **Mistake:** ❌ he is processing the playwright writing execution
- **Correction:** ✅ He is **executing** the Playwright test suite.
- **Rule:** Avoid stacking multiple action words (*processing writing execution*). Choose **one precise technical action verb** (*executing*, *running*, *writing*).

### 4. Present Perfect vs Simple Past (Time Markers)
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 5 — Tenses
- **Error Count:** 2 times
- **Mistake:** ❌ Yesterday, our team have executed all test scenarios.
- **Correction:** ✅ Yesterday, our team **executed** all test scenarios.
- **Rule:** If a specific past time word (*yesterday, last week, in 2024, 2 hours ago*) is present, use **Simple Past (V2)**, NOT Present Perfect (`have + V3`).

### 5. Helping Verbs in Negative Present Perfect
- **Severity:** `P1 — Critical`
- **Category:** Category 3 — Helping Verbs & Tenses
- **Error Count:** 1 time
- **Mistake:** ❌ The QA team not verifed playwright framework file.
- **Correction:** ✅ The QA team **has not verified** the Playwright framework file.
- **Rule:** Negative Present Perfect requires `have not / has not + V3` (`has not verified`, `have not tested`). You cannot omit `has / have`.

### 6. Definite Article 'the' with Specific Noun Phrases
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 9 — Articles
- **Error Count:** 9 times
- **Mistake:** ❌ Have you checked playwright framework file structure?
- **Correction:** ✅ Have you checked **the** Playwright framework file structure?
- **Rule:** Specific compound noun phrases (*the Playwright framework*, *the file structure*) take **the**. Capitalize tool names (*Playwright*).

### 7. Preposition after "Explain"
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 10 — Prepositions
- **Error Count:** 1 time
- **Mistake:** ❌ Explain me Taxi system architecture.
- **Correction:** ✅ Explain **the** taxi system architecture **to me**.
- **Rule:** Use `explain + [something] + to + [someone]`. You cannot say *explain me*.
