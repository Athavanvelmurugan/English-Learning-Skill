# Resolved Mistakes (Mastered)

### 1. Subject-Verb Agreement with Plural Nouns (`developers ARE`)
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 2 — Subject-Verb Agreement
- **Resolved Date:** 2026-09-08
- **Original Error:** ❌ developers is fixing...
- **Mastered Fix:** ✅ Plural nouns take **are** (`developers ARE fixing`, `engineers ARE executing`).

### 2. Infinitive Pattern (`to + base verb`)
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 4 — Verb Forms & Infinitives
- **Resolved Date:** 2026-09-08
- **Original Error:** ❌ to Execution / to automation
- **Mastered Fix:** ✅ Use `to + base verb` (`to execute`, `to automate`).

### 3. Uncountable Nouns in Software Engineering (`code`)
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 6 — Nouns (Uncountable)
- **Resolved Date:** 2026-09-04
- **Original Error:** ❌ ...git pushed the codes.
- **Mastered Fix:** ✅ ...modified the **code** (never *codes*).

### 4. Comparison Words (`than` vs `then`)
- **Severity:** `P3 — Minor`
- **Category:** Category 14 — Word Order & Comparison
- **Resolved Date:** 2026-09-04
- **Original Error:** ❌ Playwright is faster then Selenium...
- **Mastered Fix:** ✅ Playwright is faster **than** Selenium... (*than* for comparison).

### 5. Prepositions before State/Condition (`in condition`)
- **Severity:** `P2 — Important`
- **Category:** Category 10 — Prepositions
- **Resolved Date:** 2026-09-04
- **Original Error:** ❌ maintain server better condition.
- **Mastered Fix:** ✅ keep our backend server **in better condition** (*in + condition*).

### 6. Word Choice (Adverbs)
- **Severity:** `P3 — Minor`
- **Category:** Category 12 — Adverbs
- **Resolved Date:** 2026-09-04
- **Original Error:** ❌ don't delete records straightly.
- **Mastered Fix:** ✅ Don't delete database records **directly** (*directly* instead of *straightly*).
