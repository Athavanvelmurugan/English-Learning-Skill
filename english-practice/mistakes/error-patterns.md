# Recurring Error Patterns Analysis

## Pattern 1: Verb Modification after Prepositions
- **Frequency:** High (3 instances)
- **Root Cause:** Direct native language translation where infinitive ("to do") is used instead of gerund ("doing") after prepositions.
- **Remediation Plan:** Enforce `preposition + verb-ing` micro-drills in daily stand-up sentences.

## Pattern 2: Article Placement Before Action Verbs
- **Frequency:** Medium (1 instance in spontaneous typing)
- **Root Cause:** Typing speed & phonetic confusion with auxiliary verbs (`an implement` vs `am implementing`).
- **Remediation Plan:** Visual rule check: "Is the word a Noun? Yes -> a/an. No -> NO article."

## Pattern 3: Missing Helper Verb in Negative Perfect
- **Frequency:** Medium (1 instance in MCQ)
- **Root Cause:** Omitting `has/have` in negative sentences (*they not tested* instead of *they have not tested*).
- **Remediation Plan:** Equation practice: `Subject + has not / have not + V3`.
