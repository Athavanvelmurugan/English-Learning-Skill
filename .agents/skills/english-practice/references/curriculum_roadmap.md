# English Learning Curriculum & Prerequisite Roadmap

This reference document outlines the complete 6-level curriculum structure, prerequisite dependency tree, and topic mastery status definitions for the English Practice Skill.

---

# 1. Topic Status Legend

Every topic in the curriculum is assigned one of 5 statuses in `english-practice/progress/tracking.md`:

| Status Symbol | Name | Definition | Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🔴 | **Not Learned** | Prerequisite not yet unlocked or lesson not started | Initial state |
| 🟠 | **Learning** | Currently studying rules & basic examples | Active lesson in progress |
| 🟡 | **Needs Practice** | Attempted in exercises, accuracy between 50%–79% | Needs targeted drills & sentence construction |
| 🟢 | **Strong** | High accuracy (80%–84%) in MCQs and sentences | Consistent correct usage |
| 🔵 | **Mastered** | MCQ accuracy >= 85% AND Sentence accuracy >= 80% AND Workplace context usage | Fully unlocked & integrated |

# 3. Complete Level Breakdown

## Level 0 — Absolute Foundation
- **Topic 0.1:** Alphabet, Phonics & Simple Words
- **Topic 0.2:** Identifying Subject, Verb, and Object
- **Topic 0.3:** Basic Sentence Formation (`S + V`, `S + V + O`)
- **Topic 0.4:** Nouns & Pronouns (`I`, `you`, `he`, `she`, `it`, `we`, `they`)
- **Topic 0.5:** Singular vs Plural Nouns (`project` vs `projects`)

## Level 1 — Beginner Grammar
- **Topic 1.1:** Be Verbs — Present (`am`, `is`, `are`)
- **Topic 1.2:** Be Verbs — Past (`was`, `were`)
- **Topic 1.3:** Helping Verbs — `do` / `does` & `have` / `has`
- **Topic 1.4:** Articles — Indefinite (`a` / `an` before Vowel SOUNDS)
- **Topic 1.5:** Articles — Definite (`the`) & Zero Article
- **Topic 1.6:** Prepositions of Place & Time (`in`, `on`, `at`)
- **Topic 1.7:** Basic Adjectives & Adverbs
- **Topic 1.8:** Simple Negative Sentences & Questions

## Level 2 — Core Tenses
- **Topic 2.1:** Simple Present (Habits, Routines, Facts)
- **Topic 2.2:** Present Continuous (Actions happening now)
- **Topic 2.3:** Simple Past (Completed past actions with time markers)
- **Topic 2.4:** Past Continuous (Actions in progress in the past)
- **Topic 2.5:** Simple Future (`will` + base verb) & `going to`
- **Topic 2.6:** Present Perfect (Completed actions with present relevance)
- **Topic 2.7:** Present Perfect Continuous (Started in past, continuing now)
- **Topic 2.8:** Past Perfect & Past Perfect Continuous
- **Topic 2.9:** Future Perfect & Future Perfect Continuous

## Level 3 — Intermediate Grammar
- **Topic 3.1:** Modal Verbs — Ability & Permission (`can`, `could`, `may`)
- **Topic 3.2:** Modal Verbs — Advice & Obligation (`should`, `must`, `have to`)
- **Topic 3.3:** Modal Verbs — Possibility & Hypotheses (`might`, `would`)
- **Topic 3.4:** Conjunctions (`because`, `although`, `however`, `therefore`)
- **Topic 3.5:** Comparatives & Superlatives (`faster than`, `the most efficient`)
- **Topic 3.6:** Infinitives (`to + base verb`) vs Gerunds (`verb-ing`)
- **Topic 3.7:** Relative Clauses (`who`, `which`, `that`, `where`)
- **Topic 3.8:** Conditional Sentences (Zero, 1st, 2nd, 3rd)
- **Topic 3.9:** Passive Voice (`The code was tested by QA`)
- **Topic 3.10:** Reported Speech (`She said that the API was ready`)

## Level 4 — Advanced Practical English
- **Topic 4.1:** Complex Sentence Connectors & Transition Words
- **Topic 4.2:** Tone & Nuance — Hedging (`It appears that...`, `We might consider...`)
- **Topic 4.3:** Formal vs Informal Email & Slack Phrasing
- **Topic 4.4:** Practical Phrasal Verbs in Work Settings (`look into`, `follow up`, `set up`)
- **Topic 4.5:** Business Idioms (`on the same page`, `back to square one`)
- **Topic 4.6:** Professional Disagreement & Softening Conflict

## Level 5 — Professional Communication
- **Topic 5.1:** Daily Stand-Up Updates (Yesterday, Today, Blockers)
- **Topic 5.2:** Bug Escalation & Root Cause Analysis Explanations
- **Topic 5.3:** Client Demos & Architecture Explanations
- **Topic 5.4:** Technical & Behavioral Job Interviews
- **Topic 5.5:** Manager Conversations, One-on-Ones & Salary Discussions
